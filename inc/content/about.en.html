get a live was born out of a simple frustration: setting up a good-looking stream in OBS takes hours, and most overlay packs arrive as a pile of PNGs you have to fit in scene by scene.

We do it differently. Each template is a complete scene collection: starting, game, just chatting, react, be right back and ending, with animated overlays, on-screen chat, alerts with sound and Twitch panels. You download it, double-click the installer and OBS opens with everything in place.

The overlays are made with lightweight HTML, CSS and JavaScript, running as browser sources inside OBS itself. That means smooth animations, customization in a single text file (your name, your socials, your schedule) and no extra programs running alongside your stream.

And if the template of your dreams doesn't exist yet, just ask. The ideas that come in through the requests page are what decide what we make next.
